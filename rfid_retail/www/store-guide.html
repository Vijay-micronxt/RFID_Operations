How the store works

Every garment carries a small reusable RFID tag. Stock staff put the tags on when stock arrives. At the counter the cashier puts the garments on a tray, the tray reads all tags at once and POS Next makes the bill. This guide walks you through every screen.

🏷️Tag

A sticker or hang tag with a chip. Each has its own code. After a sale the same tag goes on a new garment.

📥Tray

The reader plate on the counter. Put garments on it and it reads every tag together, like a barcode scanner that sees many codes.

🕘Shift

The counter's working day in POS Next. Open it with the drawer cash in the morning, close it and count at night.

Set up the storeManager · 5 min · once

Switch RFID on

RFID Settings is the master switch and the rule book: what the counter must check, what happens to a tag after a sale, and who may use each station.

Open RFID Settings
  1. Tick Enable RFID. While it is off nothing RFID-related runs anywhere.
  2. Check the Sale rules. The defaults suit most stores (see the table).
  3. After A Sale: Free the tag at once means a sold tag can go on new stock straight away.
  4. Allow removing tags from unsold items: only if the manager should be able to take a tag off stock that was not sold.
  5. Scroll down to Station access, give each station to roles, then Save.

Stock and taggingStock staff · 4 min · every delivery

Put tags on from the document

The quickest way: tag the pieces right from the Purchase Receipt, one row at a time. Identical pieces share the row, so any tag can go on any of them.

  1. Item row: pick the row. It says how many pieces still need a tag.
  2. The counter shows tags read against pieces waiting, here 0 / 2.
  3. Put a tag on each piece and hold them all on the reader. Each code appears in Tags on the reader; a tag read twice counts once.
  4. Tag now. When the count matches, the tags save by themselves. Fewer than the row is fine; tag the rest later.
  5. Read tags on the wrong row? Check stickers / move tags moves them to the right row. No need to cancel.
Green tagFree and good. It goes on this row's pieces.
Red tagUnknown, damaged, lost or already on another garment. Take it off and use another; the save waits.

Open Tag garments

  2. Put all the tags on the reader at once. They appear one by one.

Tag history and removing tags

Read any tag to see its full story. Free the tags of sold pieces so they can be used again.

Open Tag historyOpen Remove tags
  1. Read the tag (or type its code, or scan a garment label).
  2. Its status now: Available, Assigned, Damaged, Lost…
  3. Manager actions: Damaged, Retire, or Found again for a lost tag.
  4. Garments this tag was on: each use, with the purchase it came in on and the bill it was sold on.
  5. Everything that happened: every event with time and user.
  1. Read the tags of paid pieces. Choose why: Remove - use again, Damaged or Lost.
  2. Released this session lists what was freed.
Unsold piecesA tag on a piece that was not sold is not freed here. Only a store manager can remove it, and only if RFID Settings allows it.
Stock and taggingStock staff · 5 min · daily

Daily stock count

Walk the floor with the handheld and scan every tag. The screen ticks off each garment that should be there and shows what is missing. Counting never changes stock.

Open Stock countPast counts
  1. Pick the warehouse, press Start today's count, then scan every tag (Enter after each, or let the handheld type).
  2. Expected, found complete and codes scanned update live; each garment turns green when all its pieces are scanned.
  3. Finish count & show result: Matched, Unverified, Unexpected, Unmapped, Sold but Observed, Incomplete Set.
  4. Send for review. A different person (Audit Review station) must review it.
  5. Discard this count only for a count started by mistake.
No count today?With Daily handheld count required on, a Missed Audit exception is raised at 23:00.

Know the sale screen

Items on the left, the bill on the right, the RFID tray box on top of the bill.

  1. Tray box: the tray reader types here. Keep the cursor in it while billing.
  2. Tag unreadable - scanning printed labels: tick to scan stickers instead of tags.
  3. Customer: type a name or mobile, + to create one. Leave it for walk-in.

  7. Online status, cache, printer and refresh. Press refresh if a new item does not show.

At the counterCashier · 4 min · every bill

Bill on the tray

Put the customer's garments on the tray. Each piece read goes into the cart; problems show in red under the tray box.

  1. All garments on the tray. The reader fills the box by itself.
  2. Red line = this tag cannot be sold (here: a tag not on any garment). Put that piece aside and tell the manager.
  3. Read pieces land in the cart with the right quantity. × removes a line.
  4. Total quantity, subtotal, tax and Grand Total.
  5. Checkout to take payment.
  6. Hold parks the bill as a draft if the customer steps away.
SetsA suit with a missing piece stays in an orange box above the cart, each piece marked ✓ or ✕. Payment waits until the missing piece is read, or press Remove.
OfflineAn RFID counter cannot finish a bill without internet. Wait for the connection.
At the counterCashier · 2 min

Clicking an item by hand

Tagged pieces must be read on the tray. If you click a tagged item in the item list, the POS asks first, or refuses.

  1. The POS says how many pieces of this item have a tag and how many do not.
  2. Scan the tag: the piece has a tag. Put it on the tray instead.
  3. Piece has no tag - add: only for a piece that truly has no tag. You can never add more than the untagged pieces in stock.
All pieces taggedIf every piece of the item in stock has a tag, clicking it is refused: Put it on the RFID tray instead of picking it by hand.
At the counterCashier · 3 min · every bill

Take payment

Pick how the customer pays, make Remaining zero, complete the payment and print.

  1. Payment method: UPI, Card or Cash. Split across two if needed.
  2. Quick amounts: tap the exact bill or a round note.
  3. Or type the amount on the numpad and press Add.
  4. Remaining must reach ₹ 0. Extra cash shows as change to give back.
  5. Complete Payment, then Print Invoice. The tags are freed by themselves.
  6. Offers shows discounts that apply; Coupon takes a code.
Card and UPICannot be more than the bill. Only cash can give change.

Customer returns

Find the original bill, tick what came back, choose how to refund.

  1. Search by bill number, customer or mobile.
  2. Tap the bill. Then tick the pieces and set Return Qty.
  3. A bill already returned shows Credit Note Issued. Choose the refund method, add a reason, Create Return.
After the returnHand the garment to stock staff. It shows in the station's To tag list and is checked and re-tagged before it sells again.
At the counterCashier · 4 min · every evening

Close the shift

Check the day's sales, count the money per payment method, close.

  1. Gross sales, returns, net sales and tax for the shift.
  2. Every bill and return of the shift.
  1. Actual Amount: count and type what you really have for each method.
  2. Balanced: actual equals expected.
  3. Short or Over: the difference. Recount before closing.
  4. Cash short or over is shown in money. Then Close Shift and Print EOD Report.
Do not leave it openClose every shift at night. A shift left open mixes two days of sales.

When things go wrongManager · 3 min · daily

Check the exceptions list

Everything that went wrong anywhere is logged as an RFID Exception. Look at it every day and close what you have dealt with.

Open RFID Exceptions
  1. Filter by Event, Severity or Status. Open items only by default.
  2. Event: what happened.
  3. Severity: deal with High first.
EventWhat to do
Unmapped TagA tag was read that is on no garment. Find it and tag the garment.
Barcode FallbackA sticker was scanned instead of the tag. Check the tag on that piece.
Exit AlertAn unsold garment passed the gate. Check at once.
Audit Shortage / UnexpectedFrom the stock count: find the missing or extra pieces.
Missed AuditNo stock count that day.
Tag Removed / Tag Replaced / Tag LostRecord of tag changes. Close after checking.
Untagged StockAfter a cancel the warehouse has more tags than stock. Find those tags in the next count.
Manual OverrideA manager approved a rule break. Check it was right.
